# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be a safe, respectful, and
constructive experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience level, or perspective.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:

- communicate with empathy, patience, and technical honesty;
- critique ideas and implementations rather than people;
- welcome questions and help contributors learn the project boundaries;
- respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's private information;
- acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and accept constructive feedback; and
- keep discussions relevant to the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not tolerated:

- harassment, intimidation, threats, or discriminatory language;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- personal attacks, trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberate humiliation;
- publishing private information without explicit permission;
- encouraging unsafe disclosure of credentials or vulnerabilities; or
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  open-source community.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other project spaces. It also applies when someone is officially
representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the
repository's available moderation or private-contact channels. Do not use a
public issue when doing so could expose a participant or sensitive details.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, commits, code, issues,
or other material that violates this code. Responses may include a private
warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces,
depending on severity and pattern. Reports will be reviewed in good faith with
attention to the privacy and safety of everyone involved.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by established open-source community standards,
including the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
